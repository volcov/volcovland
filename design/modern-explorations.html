D, fixed: three tones + a wooden structure

The contrast bug you spotted was real. In the squircle field and the knockout were literally the same --badge value, so the bottom half of the drop dissolved into the background and only the part above the track held the silhouette. Fixed with a third tone: deep field #3b0f75, white drop, and the below-track region in #7c3aed. The drop now closes on all sides.

A side benefit: three self-contained tones mean the badge needs no light/dark variants at all. The headers below prove it — same colours in both, unlike the current mascot which hardcodes purple and has to be re-tinted.

On the wooden structure — the honest arithmetic. The trestle lives in the purple band between the track and the bottom of the drop, which is 17–42 units deep. At 28px that is 4.8–11.8px. The posts are 5 units (1.4px) and the lattice bracing 3.5 units (1px). So the structure genuinely works at 56px and above, and genuinely does not at 28px — at header size it turns into grey haze along the underside of the track.

What's actually wrong. The mark is drawn for one size and used at another. Measured in the live header, where it renders at 28×28 from a 100×120 viewBox (scale 0.233):

  • stroke-width="2" smile and outline → 0.47px — below one device pixel, so it renders as a grey smudge
  • eye highlights r="1.5" → 0.70px — invisible
  • eyes r="4" → 1.87px — two faint specks

It also has three different centres: the tip sits at x=55, the body's widest span is centred on x=53, and the eyes are centred on x=50. That reads as wobble at large sizes and as lopsidedness at small ones. Every option below is symmetric about x=50, drops the sub-pixel highlights, and thickens what remains so it survives 28px.

Each card shows the mark at 128px, 56px and the real 28px header size, then in a light and a dark header. Judge them at 28px first — that's where it actually lives.
